# TP2-G5-A5 — Non-Circularity and Open Bridges

The synthesis is non-circular only when each object retains its declared role:

1. Exact identities are not estimates.
2. Nonnegative terms are not automatically coercive.
3. A weighted stochastic history is not an Eulerian spatial average.
4. Integrated smallness is not pointwise, pathwise, or cone control.
5. A covariance trace is not isotropy.
6. A conditional regularity criterion is not a target-equivalent proof.

The open bridge set is therefore explicit: a uniform or integrated Xi estimate based on the full coefficient fields, a history-to-space measure/coverage theorem, covariance spectral control, and a non-circular continuation implication that matches Clay Statement B.
