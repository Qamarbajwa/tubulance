# TH9 LITERATURE THEOREM COMPARISON: DYNAMICAL STRETCHING PERSISTENCE

## 1. Classical and Recent Dynamical Formulations
1. **Buaria, Bodenschatz, Pumir (2020/2024)**: Discovered that intense vortex tubes experience internal twisting/anti-twisting that suppresses nonlinear amplification.
2. **Nomura & Post (1998)**: Established that the strain eigenframe rotates in response to pressure Hessian and vorticity-induced velocity gradients.
3. **Vieillefosse (1982) & Cantwell (1992)**: Analyzed restricted Euler dynamics and showed that unconstrained self-stretching drives finite-time blowup along the Vieillefosse tail ($27R^2 + 4Q^3 = 0$), but the pressure Hessian in true Navier-Stokes enforces non-axisymmetric frame rotation.
