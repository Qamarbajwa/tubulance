# TP2-G5-A2 — Riccati Term Audit

The exact negative quadratic term is
\[
\boxed{R_\Xi=-2\nu\Xi^2.}
\]
It originates only from the denominator drift:
\[
-\frac{Nb_R}{R^2}=-\frac{N(2\nu N)}{R^2}
=-2\nu\left(\frac NR\right)^2.
\]
It does not originate from numerator dissipation. The numerator quadratic
variation is part of I_Xi, where it combines with covariance and denominator
corrections.
This is an exact negative quadratic term, not a Navier-Stokes damping theorem.
The nonnegative I_Xi term or sign-indefinite D_Xi can balance or dominate it.
Periodic shear has D_Xi=0, while I_Xi can be zero only under the stated
compatibility condition. No global decay of Xi follows.
