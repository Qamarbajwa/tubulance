# TP2-G5-A2 — Deterministic Geometry Terms

Let \(M=D^TD\). Since \(F=D(\Omega G+GA-T^S)\),
\[
D_\Xi=D_{\rm ROT}+D_{\rm ADV}+D_{\rm SOURCE},
\]
\[
D_{\rm ROT}=\frac{2}{R}\langle DG,D\Omega G\rangle_F,\quad
D_{\rm ADV}=\frac{2}{R}\langle DG,DGA\rangle_F,\quad
D_{\rm SOURCE}=-\frac{2}{R}\langle DG,DT^S\rangle_F.
\]
Equivalently,
\[
D_{\rm ADV}=\frac{2}{R}\operatorname{tr}(G^TMGA)
=\frac{2}{R}\operatorname{tr}(G^TMGS),
\]
because \(G^TMG\) is symmetric and the Omega part of A has zero trace
contraction. The rotational term does not generally vanish:
\[
D_{\rm ROT}=\frac{2}{R}\operatorname{tr}(G^TM\Omega G),
\]
which can be nonzero because M need not commute with Omega.
The source term contains \(T^S=(\nabla S)\omega\) and is higher-derivative
and sign-indefinite. All three deterministic terms are pointwise and local
in the displayed jet but frame-dependent. They survive in general under
D=I except D_ROT, which vanishes at D=I. Periodic shear has F=0 and all
three vanish. Beltrami fields retain the terms after exact field substitution;
pressure cancellation does not make D_Xi sign-definite.
No deterministic geometry term is universally favorable or unfavorable.
