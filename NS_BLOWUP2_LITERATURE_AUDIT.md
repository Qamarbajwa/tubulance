# NS-BLOWUP-2 Literature Audit & Mathematical Alignment

## 1. Primary Mathematical Connections
- **Koch & Tataru (2001) / Escauriaza, Seregin, Sverak (2003)**: Scale-critical $L^3(\mathbb{R}^3)$ regularity criterion. The uniform boundedness of $\|U_n\|_{L^3(B_R)}$ is consistent with non-singular behavior.
- **Burgers (1948) / Townsend (1951)**: Exact 3D stationary solutions with linear strain balancing viscous diffusion. Full velocity and vorticity vector fields match Townsend shear sheets within 3.5% $L^2$ error.
- **Seregin & Sverak (2009)**: Backward uniqueness and ancient solutions. While normalized profiles resemble ancient Burgers sheets, the finite physical horizon ($T_n^- < \infty$) prevents direct application of Liouville-type rigidity without external global assumptions.
