# TP2-G5-A2 — Non-Circularity Audit

The exact decomposition is
\[
b_\Xi=D_\Xi-2\nu\Xi^2+I_\Xi.
\]
Bounding D_Xi by generic operator norms introduces D, A, S, Omega, omega,
J_omega, and strain-gradient controls. Imposing L-infinity-in-time or
Serrin/BKM-level control on these fields is conditional and can be
regularity-equivalent to the Clay continuation criterion.

Classifications:
- KNOWN_CONDITIONAL: estimates under assumed bounded A, S, omega, or
  continuation norms.
- DIRECT_EVOLUTION_ROUTE_CIRCULAR: using BKM/Serrin or equivalent bounds as
  the a-priori input for a global Xi estimate.
- POTENTIALLY_NONCIRCULAR: a future pressure, commutator, geometric, or
  integrated stochastic cancellation that derives its control without those
  assumptions. No such control is proved here.

Bounded D alone is insufficient, and the positive I_Xi square does not imply
coercivity. The A2 conclusion is SIGN-INDEFINITE_BALANCE with exact
NONNEGATIVE_ITO_COMPENSATION.
