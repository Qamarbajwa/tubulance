# TP2-G5-A3 — Geometric No-Go Results

The following implications were challenged and not accepted:

1. `kappa_omega` does not equal `Xi` without deformation-metric control.
2. Direction coherence does not by itself bound `Xi`; it may address `J_xi`, but `D`, `beta`, and metric conditioning remain.
3. Sparseness and analyticity criteria do not automatically control `C_D` or `E_mu[Xi]`.
4. `E_mu[Xi]` cannot be silently replaced by an Eulerian spatial average or enstrophy dissipation.
5. `Cond(D)` bounds are conditional envelopes and become circular if obtained from the continuation norm under investigation.
6. `beta_j` is not `partial_j log|D xi|` without additional spatial differentiation of `D`.
7. The exact A2 drift factorization does not yield an A3 evolution closure: `D_Xi` requires higher derivative-order information and remains non-closed.

These are typed negative results and scope limits. They do not falsify the exact identities or rule out a future non-circular integrated, pressure-mediated, geometric, or stochastic bridge.
