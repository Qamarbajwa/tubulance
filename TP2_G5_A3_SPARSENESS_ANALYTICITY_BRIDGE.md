# TP2-G5-A3 — Sparseness and Analyticity Bridge Audit

Geometric sparseness criteria for superlevel sets and analyticity-based arguments provide conditional regularity mechanisms under their own scale, geometry, and solution-class hypotheses. The audited Grujic sparseness work uses a geometric-measure condition rather than a pointwise deformation-metric derivative estimate; it does not identify `Xi` with a sparseness parameter.

Likewise, analyticity or inverse-length arguments may control a solution after regularity and analyticity have been established, but that is not an automatic bound on `D`, `beta`, or `E_mu[Xi]` near a candidate singular time. Any bridge would need a new theorem with explicit constants, domain, stopping, and non-circular hypotheses.

Reference: [Grujic, “A geometric measure-type regularity criterion for 3D NSE”](https://arxiv.org/abs/1111.0217) and the published record [10.1088/0951-7715/26/1/289](https://doi.org/10.1088/0951-7715/26/1/289).
