# TP2-G5-A3 — Inverse-Length-Scale Comparison

`kappa_omega = ||J_omega||_F/|omega|` is a local inverse-length quantity on `|omega| > 0`. The identity

`kappa_omega^2 = |grad log|omega||^2 + ||J_xi||_F^2`

separates amplitude and direction contributions.

Audited inverse-length literature, including Gibbon’s vorticity-moment hierarchy, concerns Eulerian norms, moments, or time-averaged quantities under declared periodic or whole-space hypotheses. It does not provide the deformation-weighted numerator `||D J_omega||^2`, the metric denominator `|D omega|^2`, or the stochastic measure `mu` used by `E_mu[Xi]`.

Therefore the safe classification is `RELATED_ANALOGUE / NO_DIRECT_TRANSFER`. Similar dimensions or inverse-length language do not establish a bound on `Xi`.

Reference: [Gibbon, “The three-dimensional Euler equations: singular or non-singular?”](https://arxiv.org/abs/1012.3604).
