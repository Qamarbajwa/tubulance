# TH8 LITERATURE THEOREM COMPARISON: STRETCHING DEPLETION

## 1. Classical Strain Alignment Theorems
1. **Ashurst, Kerstein, Kerr, Gibson (1987)**: First observed empirical preferential alignment of $\boldsymbol{\omega}$ with intermediate strain eigenvector $\mathbf{e}_2$ in isotropic turbulence.
2. **Constantin (1994)**: Proved geometric depletion of the Euler singularity assuming Hölder continuity of the vorticity direction field.
3. **Gibbon (2008)**: Established $L^{2m}$ differential bounds for vorticity moments controlling growth via $\|\mathbf{S}\|_{L^\infty}$.
4. **Beale-Kato-Majda (1984)**: Regularity requires $\int_0^T \|\boldsymbol{\omega}(t)\|_{L^\infty} dt < \infty$.
