# TP2-G5-A2 — Three-Level Understanding Gate

## Level 1

Xi drifts through a sign-indefinite deterministic geometry term, an exact
negative denominator-Riccati term, and a nonnegative correlated-noise square.

## Level 2

\[
(Z,Y)\longrightarrow(R,N),\qquad
(N,R,[N,R])\longrightarrow\Xi,
\]
\[
b_\Xi=D_\Xi+R_\Xi+I_\Xi
=\frac{2\langle Y,F\rangle_F}{R}-2\nu\Xi^2
+\frac{2\nu}{R}\sum_\ell
\left\|H_\ell-\frac{2c_\ell}{R}Y\right\|_F^2.
\]
The shared Brownian coordinates generate both the covariance term and the
positive square. The deterministic geometry remains coupled to the full
Navier-Stokes jet.

## Level 3

Expand the A1 quotient coefficients; complete the three stochastic terms
componentwise; split F into rotation, advection, and strain-gradient source;
audit the Riccati origin; derive the time-dependent mu correction; calibrate
shear, Beltrami, zero-gradient, and D=I states; then classify the result as
sign-indefinite balance rather than coercivity. No pressure Hessian is
inserted directly and no A3 work is performed.
