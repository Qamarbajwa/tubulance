# TH9 REGULARITY CONNECTION: DYNAMICAL PERSISTENCE AND THE BKM INTEGRAL

## 1. The Time-Integrated Vorticity Bound
$$\ln\left(\frac{\|\boldsymbol{\omega}(T)\|_{L^\infty}}{\|\boldsymbol{\omega}(0)\|_{L^\infty}}\right) \le \int_0^T \lambda_\omega(\mathbf{x}^*(t), t) \, dt$$

## 2. Why Finite Episode Lifetime Constrains Amplification
Because near-maximal stretching episodes ($\rho > 0.90$) have bounded durations $P_3 = \int_{\text{episode}} \lambda_3 dt \le 1.5$, any sustained growth must alternate between brief bursts of stretching and longer intervals of intermediate-strain alignment ($\mathbf{e}_2$) or compression ($\mathbf{e}_1$). However, proving global regularity still requires establishing an a priori uniform bound on the number of such bursts or on $\int_0^T \lambda_3 dt$.
