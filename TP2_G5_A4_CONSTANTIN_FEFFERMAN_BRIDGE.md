# TP2-G5-A4 — Constantin–Fefferman Bridge Audit

The [Constantin–Fefferman 1993 paper](https://doi.org/10.1512/iumj.1993.42.42034) gives a geometric vorticity-direction regularity criterion, commonly expressed through Lipschitz/increment control of the direction in regions relevant to large vorticity. Related criteria use explicit integrability and direction hypotheses.

**A4 classification: NO_DIRECT_TRANSFER.** Branch R supplies only integrated \(\mu\)-weighted control of \(C_D\), a transverse derivative in the deformation metric, and of \(M_D\), a deformation-weighted amplitude-plus-coupling quantity, along stochastic histories. Neither is an unweighted pointwise derivative, an increment/Hölder bound, or high-vorticity-region coherence statement.

**YES_WITH_EXTRA_ASSUMPTIONS** would require uniform condition-number control, conversion of \(\mu\)-history estimates to Eulerian spatial estimates, temporal/spatial coverage, and a matched direction criterion. Adding BKM, Serrin, bounded strain, or already-valid analyticity as a premise is **CIRCULAR_FOR_CLAY_CLOSURE** rather than a new A4 bridge.
