# TH7 REGULARITY CONNECTION: WHY V_LOG^+ CANNOT BE BOUNDED LOCALLY WITHOUT NONLOCAL CONTROL

## 1. The Pointwise Trajectory Differential Inequality
$$\frac{D\ln|\boldsymbol{\omega}|}{Dt} = \lambda_\omega + \nu \left( \frac{\Delta|\boldsymbol{\omega}|}{|\boldsymbol{\omega}|} - \|\nabla\hat{\boldsymbol{\omega}}\|_F^2 \right)$$

## 2. The Unresolved Nonlocal Difficulty
Because $\Delta|\boldsymbol{\omega}|$ involves second spatial derivatives of velocity (third derivatives of $\mathbf{u}$), bounding $\nu \frac{\Delta|\boldsymbol{\omega}|}{|\boldsymbol{\omega}|}$ at an arbitrary interior point without assuming higher-order Sobolev bounds ($H^2$ or $C^2$) is mathematically impossible from local $L^\infty$ quantities alone. However, at the **global supremum** $\sup_{\mathbf{x}} |\boldsymbol{\omega}(\mathbf{x}, t)|$, $\Delta|\boldsymbol{\omega}| \le 0$ identically, proving that **viscous influx never amplifies the maximum vorticity of the flow**.
