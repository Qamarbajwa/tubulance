## 2. CLM-037-A: Local Homogeneous Pulse Amplification

### 2.1 Explicit Hypotheses
1. **Background Flow Field ($u_B$)**:
   - Prescribed purely affine linear velocity field $u_B(x, t) = A(t) x$ on $\mathbb{R}^3$, where $A(t) \in \mathbb{R}^{3 \times 3}$ is smooth, trace-free ($\operatorname{tr}(A(t)) = \operatorname{div} u_B = 0$), and admits an axisymmetric hyperbolic straining structure with strain rate $S(t) = \frac{1}{2}(A + A^T) = \operatorname{diag}(-2\sigma(t), \sigma(t), \sigma(t))$.
   - In self-similar time $\tau = 1 - t \in [\tau_j, \tau_{j-1}]$ with $\tau_j = 2^{-j}$, the singular strain rate satisfies $\sigma(t) \asymp \tau^{-1-h}$ with $h \in (0, 1/6)$.
2. **Time Interval**:
   - Strictly restricted to the local active burst interval $[t_j^-, t_j]$ of duration $\Delta t_j = t_j - t_j^- \asymp 2^{-j}$.
3. **Spatial Coordinates**:
   - Local rescaled variables $\xi = (x - X_j(t)) / \tau^{1/2}$, where $X_j(t)$ is the trajectory of the carrier filament.
4. **Governing Pulse Evolution**:
   - Linearized Navier–Stokes equation around $u_B$ for an isolated, divergence-free Fourier-Kelvin mode $w(x, t) = a(t) e^{i k(t) \cdot x}$:
     $$\dot{k}(t) = -A(t)^T k(t), \qquad \dot{a}(t) = \left( \frac{2 k(t) k(t)^T}{|k(t)|^2} - I \right) A(t) a(t) - \nu |k(t)|^2 a(t).$$
5. **Carrier Frequency & Wavevector Alignment**:
   - At terminal time $t_j$, the wavevector satisfies $|k_j(t_j)| = k_j^\star \asymp 2^{j/2}$.
   - The wavevector $k_j(t)$ is strictly parallel to the backward-contracting compressive axis $e_1$ of the strain tensor ($k_j(t) = k_{j, 1}(t) e_1$).
6. **Amplitude & Energy**:
   - Terminal amplitude is scale-critical: $A_j^\star \asymp 2^{j(1/2+h)}$.
   - Local kinetic energy vanishes: $\mathcal{E}(W_j^\star) = \frac{1}{2} \|W_j^\star\|_{L^2}^2 \asymp 2^{j(3h - 0.5)} \to 0$ as $j \to \infty$ for all $h < 1/6$.

### 2.2 Formal Statement of CLM-037-A
Under Hypotheses 2.1, the backward carrier wavevector contracts from $|k_j(t_j)| = k_j^\star \asymp 2^{j/2}$ to $|k_j(t_j^-)| \le K_0 = \mathcal{O}(1)$ over the burst duration $\Delta t_j$, bounding cumulative viscous dissipation on the central ray to:
$$\mathcal{D}_j = \nu \int_{t_j^-}^{t_j} |k_j(s)|^2 ds \le \nu(K_0^2 + 1) = \mathcal{O}(1),$$
while cumulative strain amplification diverges super-exponentially:
$$\mathcal{G}_j = \int_{t_j^-}^{t_j} \sigma(s) ds \asymp 2^{jh} \to +\infty.$$
Consequently, the on-axis net growth exponent $\mathcal{A}_j = \mathcal{G}_j - \mathcal{D}_j \asymp 2^{jh} \to +\infty$ is strictly proved.

## 3. CLM-037-B: Two-Family Local Stress-Cone Realization

### 3.1 Explicit Hypotheses
1. **Helical Polarization Families**:
   - The pulse field consists of two distinct helical wave families $w_+(x, t)$ and $w_-(x, t)$ with wavevectors $k_+, k_-$ and complex polarization vectors $b_+, b_-$ satisfying $k_\pm \cdot b_\pm = 0$ and $|b_\pm| = 1$.
2. **Averaged Reynolds Stress Tensor**:
   - The Reynolds stress generated by each family over one fast oscillation period $\mathbb{T}_{osc} = 2\pi / |k|$ is given by the spatial dyadic average:
     $$Q_\pm = \langle w_\pm \otimes w_\pm \rangle = \frac{1}{2} \operatorname{Re}(b_\pm \otimes \overline{b_\pm}).$$
3. **Stress Cone Definition ($K$)**:
   - Let $C(b) = \frac{1}{2} \operatorname{Re}(b \otimes \overline{b}) - \frac{1}{6} I \in \operatorname{Sym}_0(3)$ denote the deviatoric Reynolds stress.
   - The local active stress cone is the positive cone in the 2D subspace spanned by the two families:
     $$K = \left\{ y_+ C(b_+) + y_- C(b_-) \in \operatorname{Sym}_0(3) : y_+ > 0, y_- > 0 \right\}.$$
4. **Non-Degenerate Coupling Matrix**:
   - In a 2D projection onto target core stresses $T = (T_r, T_z)^T$, the coupling matrix:
     $$H = \begin{pmatrix} C_{11}(b_+) & C_{11}(b_-) \\ C_{12}(b_+) & C_{12}(b_-) \end{pmatrix} \in \mathbb{R}^{2 \times 2}$$
     satisfies $\det(H) \ne 0$.

### 3.2 Formal Statement of CLM-037-B
Under Hypotheses 3.1, for any prescribed target stress tensor $T \in \operatorname{int}(K)$, there exists a unique pair of strictly positive weights $y = (y_+, y_-)^T = H^{-1} T > 0$ such that the superposition $w = \sqrt{y_+} w_+ + \sqrt{y_-} w_-$ generates the target deviatoric stress:
$$\langle w \otimes w \rangle_{\mathrm{dev}} = T.$$
The two families are linearly independent in stress space ($H$ is invertible) and realize the required interior cone landing locally.

## 4. Boundary Specification: What is NOT in CLM-037

The following claims are **strictly excluded** from `CLM-037`:
1. **Global Torus Extension**: No claim is made that $u_B$ can be extended as a smooth periodic vector field on $\mathbb{T}^3$ while preserving the uniform linear strain structure globally.
2. **Backward Parabolic Solvability to $t = 0$**: No claim is made that the backward operator $(\partial_t + u_B \cdot \nabla - \nu \Delta)^{-1}$ is bounded on $L^2(\mathbb{T}^3)$ or $H^m(\mathbb{T}^3)$ off the central ray.
3. **Spatial Localization**: Neither `CLM-037-A` nor `CLM-037-B` accounts for the finite physical envelope $\chi(x)$ required to localize the pulse into an annular filament.
4. **Simultaneous Two-Wave De-Shearing**: No claim is made that both wavevectors $k_+$ and $k_-$ can simultaneously contract backward under the same strain tensor $S(t)$.
5. **Nonlinear Contraction**: No claim is made that the nonlinear perturbation $v = u - (u_B + w)$ contracts to zero under the unforced Navier–Stokes flow.
6. **Clay Statement B**: Neither sub-claim establishes existence of a smooth unforced initial datum $u_0 \in C^\infty(\mathbb{T}^3)$ generating blowup.
