# TP2-G5-A1 — Exact-Flow Calibration

## Periodic shear

For u=(U(y,t),0,0),
\[
A=U_yE_{12},\quad \omega=-U_y e_3,\quad
G=-U_{yy}E_{32},\quad D=I+\alpha E_{12}.
\]
Since E12 E32=0,
\[
Y=DG=G,\quad R=U_y^2,\quad N=U_{yy}^2,\quad
\Xi=U_{yy}^2/U_y^2\quad(U_y\ne0).
\]
Also GA=0 and K^A=0, so B_Y=0, while only the y-noise coefficient
C_2=-U_{yyy}E_{32} survives. The R equation reduces exactly to the
one-dimensional heat-history identity.

## Beltrami and ABC-type single shell

For curl u_0=lambda u_0, u=exp(-nu lambda^2 t)u_0 is an exact unforced
Navier-Stokes solution. The nonlinear term is pressure-absorbed. This tests
the full A,S,Omega,T^S ordering without asserting a universal Xi sign or
size theorem.

## Strained sheet, Burgers tube, and zero-gradient

Strained-sheet and Burgers-tube profiles calibrate T^S, matrix order, and
spatial-noise terms; no universal transfer is claimed. At G=0 and omega
nonzero, Y=N=Xi=0 and log Xi is forbidden. The D_K examples from A0 are
algebraic mutation tests only, not dynamical NS evidence.
