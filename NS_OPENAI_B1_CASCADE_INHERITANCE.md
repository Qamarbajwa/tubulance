## 2. Nonlinear Triad Mechanics: Generation of the Second Harmonic

Let pulse $j$ be at its peak active burst during epoch $I_j = [t_j - \Delta t_j, t_j]$:
$$w_j(x, t) \approx A_j^\star \rho_j(x) e_j(t) \cos(k_j^\star \cdot x + \phi_j(t)).$$

The nonlinear Navier–Stokes convective term generates self-interaction:
$$(w_j \cdot \nabla) w_j = \frac{1}{2} (A_j^\star)^2 \rho_j^2 (e_j \cdot k_j^\star) \dots + \frac{1}{2} (A_j^\star)^2 \left( e_j \cdot \nabla \rho_j \right) \cos^2(k_j^\star \cdot x + \phi_j) - \frac{1}{2} (A_j^\star)^2 \rho_j^2 (e_j \cdot \nabla) \left[ \sin(2 k_j^\star \cdot x + 2\phi_j) \right].$$

Applying the trigonometric identity $\cos^2(\theta) = \frac{1 + \cos(2\theta)}{2}$:
$$(w_j \cdot \nabla) w_j = \underbrace{\frac{1}{4} (A_j^\star)^2 \nabla \cdot (\rho_j^2 e_j \otimes e_j)}_{\text{Zero-Frequency Mean Reynolds Stress } Q_j} + \underbrace{\mathcal{H}_{2, j}(x, t) \cos(2 k_j^\star \cdot x + 2\phi_j)}_{\text{Second Harmonic Daughter Mode}}.$$

### 2.1 Wavevector Matching
The carrier wavevector of the daughter mode is:
$$k_{\text{daughter}} = 2 k_j^\star.$$
Its wavenumber magnitude is:
$$|k_{\text{daughter}}| = 2 |k_j^\star| \asymp 2 \cdot 2^{j/2} = 2^{(j+2)/2} = \sqrt{2} \cdot 2^{(j+1)/2} \asymp 2^{(j+1)/2} = |k_{j+1}^\star|!$$
The second harmonic generated by pulse $j$ possesses **precisely the carrier wavenumber required by pulse $j+1$**!

### 2.2 Amplitude of the Seed
The daughter mode is pumped by the source term $(w_j \cdot \nabla)w_j$ over the burst duration $\Delta t_j = 2^{-j}$:
$$a_{\text{daughter}}(t_j) \asymp \|(w_j \cdot \nabla) w_j\|_{L^\infty} \cdot \Delta t_j \asymp (A_j^\star)^2 |k_j^\star| \Delta t_j \asymp \left( 2^{j(1/2+h)} \right)^2 \cdot 2^{j/2} \cdot 2^{-j} = 2^{j(1/2 + 2h)}.$$

Since $2^{j(1/2 + 2h)} \gg 1$, the nonlinear burst of pulse $j$ produces a **macroscopic, non-zero seed** at frequency $k_{j+1}^\star$!
This daughter mode does not need to be preloaded at $t = 0$; it is birthed dynamically by the violent death of pulse $j$.
