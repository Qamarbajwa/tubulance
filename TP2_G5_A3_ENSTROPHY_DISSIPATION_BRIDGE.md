# TP2-G5-A3 — Enstrophy and Dissipation Bridge Audit

The local amplitude–direction identity gives

`q^2 kappa_omega^2 = ||grad omega||_F^2`.

For a smooth incompressible Navier–Stokes solution, the viscous enstrophy dissipation contribution is an Eulerian spatial integral of `||grad omega||_F^2`, with the conventional viscosity factor and domain measure. This is distinct from the pathwise deformation-weighted quantity `Xi`.

The only exact expectation statement established here is the algebraic split, when the measure is defined:

`E_mu[Xi] = E_mu[M_D] + E_mu[C_D]`.

No exact identity was found that replaces `E_mu[Xi]` by an Eulerian spatial average, an Eulerian enstrophy integral, or a dissipation rate. Such a replacement would require a separately proved measure-identification relation between the stochastic deformation-weighted measure and the Eulerian measure, including support, Jacobian, and integrability control.

This is a negative bridge result, not a claim that all compensated or integrated bridges are impossible.
