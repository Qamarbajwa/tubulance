## A3 conclusion

Coherence can be relevant to `J_xi`, but a proposition connecting coherence to `C_D` or `Xi` must also control the deformation metric and the relevant derivative transport. Known criteria cannot be silently promoted to control of `Xi`. Clay Statement B remains `UNRESOLVED`.
