# TP2-G5-A4 — Adversarial Exact-Flow Calibration

These calibrations test interpretation, not singularity behavior.

* **Periodic shear / Couette:** spatially uniform vorticity has \(J_\omega=0\), hence \(\Xi=0\), \(J=0\), and \(\chi=1\). This is an exact smooth Navier–Stokes calibration showing that viscosity alone does not force decoherence.
* **Beltrami / ABC-type exact families:** smooth decaying eigenflows can have nontrivial three-dimensional deformation while stretching diagnostics are depleted or special. They calibrate that rich deformation is not the same as a lower bound on \(\Xi\).
* **High-frequency smooth families:** finite smooth fields can make spatial gradients large while the finite window and stochastic weight remain a separate issue. Large local derivatives do not establish a blow-up sequence.
* **Strong anisotropic kinematics:** \(D_K=\operatorname{diag}(K,1,K^{-1})\), \(\omega=e_1\), and \(J_\omega=e_3\otimes e_2\) give \(|D_K\omega|^2=K^2\) and \(\|D_KJ_\omega\|_F^2=K^{-2}\). This is **KINEMATIC**, not an exact Navier–Stokes joint-jet realization.

The examples can realize small or zero \(J\), \(\chi\) near one, anisotropic covariance, and small geometric pieces. No example is upgraded to a singular solution or used as a universal counterexample to the exact stochastic identity.
