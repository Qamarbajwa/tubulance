# TP2-G5-A3 — TH9-R Resurrection Audit

TH9-R is `PARTIALLY_REUSED`, not reactivated as a proof pathway.

Reusable material:

- directional frames and orientation diagnostics;
- the warning that magnitude-only deformation information is insufficient;
- explicit separation of frame geometry from scalar stretching.

Non-transferable material:

- a finite-time deformation singular frame is not the instantaneous strain eigenframe;
- `D^T D` need not commute with the antisymmetric rotation tensor;
- A3’s `beta_j` is a deformation-metric direction coupling, not an ordinary spatial derivative;
- a TH9-R coherence criterion does not imply a bound on `C_D`, `M_D`, or `Xi`.

The resurrection is therefore a literature/geometry comparison only. It does not authorize A4 or promote a regularity claim.
