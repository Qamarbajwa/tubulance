## 2. Principle of Absolute Epistemic Separation

$$\boxed{ \text{Correctness } \neq \text{ Novelty } \neq \text{ Significance } \neq \text{ Understanding } \neq \text{ Internal Verification } \neq \text{ External Validation } \neq \text{ Community Acceptance} }$$

1. **Proof Correctness** answers: *Is the argument mathematically sound under the declared hypotheses?*
2. **Novelty** answers: *Has this mathematical fact appeared previously in the literature?*
3. **Significance** answers: *Does this result structurally advance the field or resolve an essential barrier?*
4. **Understanding** answers: *Is the underlying geometric/physical mechanism transparent and explainable across three distinct abstraction levels?*
5. **Project Canonicalization** answers: *Has the claim passed all internal reproducible machine tests, independent E4 verifier audits, and regression checks?*
6. **Field Canonicalization** answers: *Has the claim been scrutinized, verified, and absorbed by the external human mathematical community?*

## 5. Hardened Novelty and Significance Ladders

### 5.1 Novelty Ladder
Internal AI search can **never** assign absolute novelty (`N3` or `N4`). The state `NOT_FOUND_IN_AUDITED_SOURCES` is strictly classified as `N2`:
- `N0_KNOWN_FOUNDATION`: Established literature baseline with verified citations.
- `N1_PROJECT_REFORMULATION`: Internal reformulation, tensor reorganization, or notation mapping of known literature.
- `N2_NOT_FOUND_IN_AUDITED_SOURCES`: Not found in audited databases (requires documented query scope, databases searched, date, and closest analogues).
- `N3_EXTERNAL_NOVELTY_SUPPORTED`: External human specialist literature review supports novelty.
- `N4_COMMUNITY_RECOGNIZED_AS_NOVEL`: Broad peer-reviewed mathematical community consensus recognizes original novelty.

### 5.2 Significance Ladder
- `S0_TECHNICAL_STEP`: Local technical computation, algebraic rearrangement, or helper identity.
- `S1_USEFUL_LEMMA`: Useful structural estimate or transport equation.
- `S2_MAJOR_PROJECT_RESULT`: Crucial no-go theorem, scale-critical rate identity, or persistent barrier closure.
- `S3_POTENTIAL_FIELD_SIGNIFICANCE`: Potential broader mathematical physics significance outside the project.
- `S4_COMMUNITY_RECOGNIZED_PARADIGM_CLOSURE`: External community consensus recognizes a major paradigm breakthrough.
