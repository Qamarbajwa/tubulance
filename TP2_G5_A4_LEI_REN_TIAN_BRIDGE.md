# TP2-G5-A4 — Lei–Ren–Tian Bridge Audit

The primary 2025 source, [Lei–Ren–Tian, *A geometric characterization of potential Navier–Stokes singularities*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.08976), proves a conditional regularity statement: vorticity directions contained in a double cone in high-vorticity regions imply regularity. Its contrapositive scenario reading is that a potential singularity cannot satisfy that cone hypothesis; the source describes this as directions not avoiding any great circle.

**A4 classification: NO_DIRECT_TRANSFER.** Small \(C_D\) controls a deformation-metric transverse derivative only on \(\mu\)-weighted stochastic histories and only after time integration. The Lei–Ren–Tian hypothesis is spatial/high-vorticity-region geometry, with its own local flux and suitable-weak-solution framework. A4 proves no map from the former to the latter.

**YES_WITH_EXTRA_ASSUMPTIONS** is possible only if one separately supplies history-to-space measure identification, quantitative lower bounds on \(|D\xi|\), uniform-in-history derivative/increment control, and a covering/propagation argument over the entire high-vorticity region. Those are not A4 consequences and may be continuation-critical.
